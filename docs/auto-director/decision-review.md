## What changes in the working contract

Preparation, readiness, recommendation timing and execution permission need distinct lifetimes. For example: while Program is held for a long sermon shot, Alfie may prepare Preview immediately. A prepared composition can remain useful longer than five seconds. Its live frame and identity readiness are re-evaluated; an expired dispatch permit is discarded, not renewed invisibly. This retains stale-work safety without repeatedly reapplying presets or starting preparation only when the operator wants to cut.

`ProgramTake.swift` / `TakeRules` and `ShowCoordinator.take` remain the manual technical gate. `ChannelFrame.swift` / `ChannelRevisions` and `ProgramRouter.routeGeneration` bind actual source/shot/route state. Proposed director intent lifetime must not replace these. Sol’s `DirectorShotPolicy.choose` currently applies minimum shot duration before choosing a candidate; future integration must decide whether that call proposes preparation or recommends a cut. It cannot safely mean both.

For A1, keep the conservative rule that Take pauses director for the first rehearsal. Read-only navigation is not an override. Test whether Take followed by Resume makes Auto Prepare worthwhile versus manual R2; if not, consider a separately chosen, explicitly labelled continuation action later. Do not quietly exempt ordinary Take from revocation. Existing Program tracking is not new director authority.

For R2/P2, define three distinct events in the next implementation specification: temporary evidence unavailable (withdraw readiness, do not choose another person); declared identity/subject loss (latch director pause); source/output fault (latch pause and retire affected generations). Evidence returning can restore a readiness calculation only while an unchanged grant remains valid; it never clears a latched pause. Timing thresholds for declaring loss need labelled replay evidence, not new arbitrary constants in this review.

Pin currently inhibits preparation and Take exactly like Pause, while Auto Prepare cannot Take anyway. Defer the UI/control until a distinct operator need is demonstrated. Preserve the future semantics if retained: editorial intent, not frozen pixels; no suppression of health faults; unpin does not resume automatically.

## Hardware evidence affecting H1

I would narrow my earlier actuator recommendation. The [Actuonix L12 datasheet](https://www.actuonix.com/assets/images/datasheets/ActuonixL12Datasheet.pdf), accessed 2026-09-30, lists a 20% maximum duty cycle and 55 dB at 45 cm, and identifies potentiometer feedback and end-of-stroke switches as separate variants. These are manufacturer specifications, not measurements of Alfie’s installation. They make duty, acoustic noise, travel geometry and independent limits selection criteria rather than details to discover after choosing the mechanism. No claim is made that this device is unsuitable without those measurements.

The [Sony SDK page](https://support.d-imaging.sony.co.jp/app/sdk/en/index.html), accessed 2026-09-30, lists PXW-Z200 and macOS support. It does not establish the required model-specific controls or a local safety deadline. Inventory an existing camera/head before choosing between protocol control and a custom mechanism; do not implement both. Keep H1 open while the no-motion simulator and safety-contract work continue.

## Voice evidence and a safer trial order

Apple documents hardware-dependent [SpeechTranscriber availability](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/speechtranscriber/isavailable) and system-managed, automatically updated [AssetInventory assets](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/assetinventory) (accessed 2026-09-30). Therefore record OS/locale/available asset information and rerun the command corpus after meaningful updates; do not promise an exact model hash if the API does not expose one. Warm installed assets before service and measure system speech-service resource use.

Push-to-talk first is a proposed trial choice, not a claim of measured lower error on this rig. It establishes an explicit operator activation boundary while recognizer/grammar performance is being measured. A wake word alone cannot distinguish an operator instruction from the same spoken words played by a loudspeaker. Shadow testing should include exact replayed commands, not just random sermon text. Hands-free operation may still be the eventual product if qualified.
